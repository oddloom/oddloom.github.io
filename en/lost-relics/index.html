How to play

  • Drag a jewel sideways to swap it with a neighboring jewel.
  • Match three or more of the same kind. Larger matches create Arrow Jewels, Seal Jewels, Solar Orbs, and the Blazing Sun.
  • Complete the level goal before you run out of moves. Goals include collecting jewels, clearing sand, breaking rocks, lowering chests, and defeating temple creatures.
  • Your progress stays on your device and can be moved with a portable save code.
